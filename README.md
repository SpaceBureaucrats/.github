# Space Bureaucrats

Giant things require a lot of time. Every day, piece by piece, they are created.

As long as you did something meaningful during the day — even something as simple as making your bed — you added another piece to the tower. Small actions may seem insignificant on their own, but over time, they build something much greater. And at the end, you will look with the great feeling of success on the created city.

*From the Ground to the Stars.*<br>
Glory to Space Bureaucrats!
